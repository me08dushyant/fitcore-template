# Design Brief — Premium Fitness Club Homepage

## 1. Project overview

**Project:** Premium Fitness Club website  
**Deliverable:** Homepage design direction and implementation-ready content blueprint  
**Brand name:** Not decided  
**Audience:** Men and women  
**Positioning:** Premium fitness club  
**Primary conversion goals:** Membership enquiries, free-trial requests and gym-tour bookings.

### Core brand statement

**This isn't just a gym. It's a place to build a stronger version of yourself.**

The website should feel like a high-end fitness brand crossed with an editorial campaign and a modern digital studio website.

## 2. Brand personality

- Confident, not aggressive.
- Premium, not intimidating.
- Modern, not overly technical.
- Inclusive and welcoming to men and women.
- Performance-focused, with a strong lifestyle element.

## 3. Art direction

### Visual language
- Cinematic athlete portraits and action photography.
- Dramatic orange/red edge lighting against deep black.
- Oversized display typography.
- Asymmetric editorial grids and overlapping imagery.
- Rounded cards and pill-shaped calls to action.
- Alternating dark and warm light sections to create rhythm.

### Photography direction
Use:
- Close-up athlete portraits.
- Men and women in athletic environments.
- Dramatic orange/red rim lighting.
- Deep shadows and black backgrounds.
- Strength-training action shots.
- Architectural gym interiors.
- Authentic expressions and believable training moments.

Avoid generic smiling fitness stock photography, overly staged bodybuilding poses,
and visuals that make the club feel intimidating or exclusively for one gender.

## 4. Color palette

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Obsidian Black | `#090909` | Primary background |
| Electric Orange | `#FF4B12` | Main accent, CTA, highlights |
| Flame Orange | `#F4511E` | Secondary accent and gradients |
| Warm Ivory | `#F5F0E8` | Light sections and cards |
| Charcoal | `#292522` | Secondary surfaces |
| Muted Stone | `#B8B0A8` | Secondary text |

Orange should be used selectively for emphasis rather than applied to every component.

## 5. Typography

Suggested type pairing:
- Display: Anton or Bebas Neue.
- Headings: Space Grotesk or Sora.
- Body: Inter.

Typography should be a major visual feature. Use short, confident headlines, large display
sizes, tight line-height and carefully controlled line breaks.

## 6. Homepage structure

Recommended visual progression:

1. Hero — Built for More
2. The Experience — More Than a Gym
3. Training Programs — Find Your Strength
4. Expert Trainers — The Right People
5. Membership — Your Goals, Your Membership
6. Community & Results — Stronger Together
7. Final CTA — Your Next Chapter Starts Here
8. Footer

### Section 01 — Hero: Built for More

**Background:** Deep black  
**Layout:** Minimal navigation, large typography on the left, cinematic athlete image on the
center/right, orange glow behind the subject, floating proof points and overlapping text.

**Headline:** BUILT FOR MORE.  
**Supporting copy:** Train with purpose. Build strength. Discover what you're capable of.  
**Primary CTA:** Explore Membership  
**Secondary CTA:** Discover Our Club

Suggested proof-point placeholders:
- `[500+]` Active Members
- `[15+]` Expert Trainers
- `[100%]` Commitment

Use real, verified figures before launch. The athlete image should overlap the headline,
echoing the supplied references.

### Section 02 — The Experience

**Background:** Warm ivory  
**Headline:** MORE THAN A GYM. A BETTER WAY TO MOVE.  
**Copy:** A space designed to challenge your limits, support your progress and make every
workout count.

Three experience highlights:
- **Premium Equipment:** Modern equipment designed for effective training.
- **Expert Guidance:** Professional trainers who understand your goals.
- **A Better Environment:** An inclusive atmosphere built around motivation and consistency.

Use a large architectural gym image paired with smaller information cards.

### Section 03 — Training Programs

**Background:** Black  
**Headline:** FIND YOUR OWN STRENGTH.

Use editorial image cards with different sizes and image treatments:
- **Strength Training:** Build strength, power and confidence.
- **Personal Training:** Individual coaching tailored to your goals.
- **Functional Fitness:** Improve mobility, balance and performance.
- **Cardio & Conditioning:** Build endurance and improve fitness.

Each card should include a large program name, image, short description and arrow.
Use subtle image zoom and orange hover details.

### Section 04 — Expert Trainers

**Background:** Warm ivory  
**Headline:** THE RIGHT PEOPLE. THE RIGHT GUIDANCE.

Show 3–4 trainer profiles. Each profile can include:
- Portrait
- Name
- Specialization
- Experience
- Short introduction

Use editorial portrait treatments rather than a conventional staff directory.
Only publish verified qualifications and experience.

### Section 05 — Membership Plans

**Background:** Black  
**Headline:** YOUR GOALS. YOUR MEMBERSHIP.

Proposed tiers:
- **Starter:** Essential gym access.
- **Plus:** More personalized training.
- **Elite:** Premium coaching experience.

These are concept structures, not confirmed package inclusions. Confirm pricing,
benefits, duration and terms with the gym before publishing. If prices are shown,
use INR for an India-based club.

Visually emphasize the Plus tier with an orange surface; keep the other cards charcoal.

### Section 06 — Community & Results

**Background:** Warm ivory  
**Headline:** STRONGER TOGETHER.

Include:
- Member testimonials.
- Member journeys and transformation stories, with consent.
- Community training photographs.
- Instagram gallery.

A horizontally scrolling editorial image gallery can create movement and visual interest.
Avoid unrealistic transformation claims or guaranteed outcomes.

### Section 07 — Final CTA

**Background:** Orange-to-red gradient transitioning into black  
**Headline:** YOUR NEXT CHAPTER STARTS HERE.  
**Copy:** Your strongest days are ahead. Take the first step today.  
**Primary CTA:** Join the Club  
**Secondary CTA:** Book a Free Gym Tour

### Footer

Include:
- Brand mark and short positioning statement.
- About, Training, Trainers and Membership links.
- Address and opening hours.
- Phone and WhatsApp contact.
- Instagram and other active social channels.
- Privacy policy and terms.

## 7. Navigation

Desktop:
- Brand mark on the left.
- About, Training, Trainers and Membership navigation.
- Contrasting orange `Join Now` CTA.

Mobile:
- Compact logo.
- Join Now CTA.
- Hamburger menu.

Keep the navigation minimal and make the membership action easy to find.

## 8. Interaction and motion

| Element | Interaction |
|---|---|
| Hero | Slow image scale and text reveal |
| Headings | Scroll-triggered reveal |
| Athlete images | Subtle parallax |
| Program cards | Image zoom on hover |
| Statistics | Count-up animation |
| Navigation | Smooth sticky navigation |
| Membership cards | Orange hover transition |
| Gallery | Horizontal drag/scroll |

Motion should be smooth and restrained. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and avoid effects
that interfere with reading or mobile performance.

## 9. Layout rhythm

Recommended section sequence:

**Black → Orange/Black → Ivory → Black → Ivory → Black → Orange/Black**

Do not make every section black. The alternating surfaces create visual rhythm and keep
the long homepage from becoming monotonous.

## 10. Responsive behavior

- Build mobile layouts deliberately rather than simply shrinking desktop sections.
- Stack asymmetric grids into a clear vertical sequence.
- Keep hero text readable and avoid covering the subject's face.
- Use appropriately cropped mobile images.
- Keep touch targets comfortably large.
- Avoid hover-only interactions on touch devices.
- Test navigation, forms and WhatsApp actions on mobile.

## 11. Conversion strategy

For an India-based gym:
- Make WhatsApp enquiries a prominent conversion route.
- Offer a free-trial request or gym-tour booking if the business supports it.
- Show location, opening hours and contact details clearly.
- Publish membership prices only if the club wants transparent public pricing.
- Use INR for published prices.
- Keep forms short: name, phone/WhatsApp, preferred goal and preferred visit time.

## 12. Content and factual safeguards

The following are placeholders until confirmed:
- Gym name and logo.
- Member and trainer counts.
- Trainer qualifications and experience.
- Equipment and facility claims.
- Membership pricing and benefits.
- Address, opening hours and contact details.
- Testimonials and transformation images.

Obtain permission before publishing identifiable member photographs or testimonials.
